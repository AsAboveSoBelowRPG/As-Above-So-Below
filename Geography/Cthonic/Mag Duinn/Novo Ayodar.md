Built primarily from the black stone that fell from the surface after the [[Primordial Gods|Primordial]] [[Nazerack]] descended from the [[Upper Sphere]], Novo Ayodar is a reconstruction of a fortress that was destroyed by the horrible God as it plunged into the [[Cthonic Remains]]. This work was orchestrated by the knight [[Afonso Roche]], who claimed to hail from these ruins.

After the death of Duque [[Ronaldo Sintaflores]], ownership of the [[Spion Isolte]] was given to his most trusted defender. Over the course of decades the knight gathered laborers to help him construct the fortress, which now stands above all other man-made structures in [[Mag Duinn]].

# Location

It was once thought that no civilization could be sustained above the [[Svata Flats]], but Lord Afonso was adamant that his stronghold be constructed in the place where its ruins lie, arguing that the ruins prove that his home once sat there exactly, and so shall it be built again.

Under the guidance of his most trusted advisor and head architect, [[Casey Gobán]], the castle managed to come together using locally quarried stone to account for the expanded blueprints and materials lost during the cataclysm. Due to its precarious geographic location, it is naturally protected and, even if faced with a siege, the walls would be too difficult to prentrate.

Access to the castle can be achieved by taking the lift to the Novo Ayodar stop, which is the last on the line, and heading north through the village of Svarta. 

# Surface Analog

Novo Ayodar was built to specifications of a terrestrial fortress known as [[Castelo Ayodar]]. Though a rather insignificant stronghold in the kingdom of [[Gallacão]]'s [[Lordless Lands]], it does hold an historic significance, in that it was the home of [[Demigods|Demigod]] necromancer [[Zyweith the Hollow]]. Zyweith was confronted by a knight from the [[Ordem Etérea]] named [[Afonso Roche]]. It was during this confrontation that the necromancer called down [[Nazerack]], bringing the calamity to [[Mag Duinn]].

This story, which is hardly notable even in the upper sphere, was unknown in the Cthonic realm until the Calamity, when tale of the confrontation began to spread in the taverns of [[Ard Breahnhelle]]. While many have made the connection to Lord Afonso of Novo Ayodar, most assume that he was named for the historic figure, and not the walking embodiment of the same man.
