Ard Breahnhelle is the largest of the [[Upper Plateau]] provinces, as well as the highest in elevation. Despite its massive size, it has a proportionally small population due to the extreme conditions and rugged geography of the region. Historically, this province has been ruled by the Sintaflores bloodline, though it is currently being contested by a former knight of the house, [[Afonso Roche]].

# Geography

Breahnhelle is accessible only via ancient lifts that are situated along the cliffside. There are stations in the Vale de Raiz, the Upper Plateau, [[Casa da Lua Culpada]], and [[Novo Ayodar]]. The population is primarily divided into three central areas near the stations. The rest of the province is rocky and mountainous with a plethora of dangerous beasts and harsh conditions.

# Population

The citizens of the province are generally split between three regions: the poorest and most rugged of which live in the cliffside dwellings along the base of [[Spion Isolte]], with the largest community being in [[Svata Flats]] just outside the Novo Ayodar lift station.

Most of the population dwells in [[Aldeia Breahnhelle]], the village attached to the Upper Plateau lift station. Because it commands the most fertile land, it has been able to grow exponentially compared to its neighbors - however, most of the food grown here is paid as tax to the nobility.

Those of means have settled the minor peaks above Aldeia, building extravagant manors on impossible perches along the cliffside. The most difficult the construction, the more social clout you hold. Though these estates are scattered among a number of inaccessible heights, they are connected by bridges, stairs and makeshift roads. The largest and most elegant of these is [[Casa da Lua Culpada]], the seat of the Sintaflores bloodline. 

# Political Tension

A former knight in the service of the deceased Duque [[Ronaldo Sintaflores]] has established a stronghold in the impenetrable heights of Spion Isolte. Though he has made no moves to challenge Duquesa [[Catarina Sintaflores]] for leadership of the province, the larger his army grows, the more the people feel the rising tensions of a looming war.

Each year at [[The Lament Masquerade]] the nobles of Breahnhelle cast their allegiances anonymously by wearing the colors of the lord they support to the fundraiser. Those who are loyal to the Sintaflores bloodline wear shades of green while those who support the opposing [[Afonso Roche]] wear blacks and reds: the colors of his army.
