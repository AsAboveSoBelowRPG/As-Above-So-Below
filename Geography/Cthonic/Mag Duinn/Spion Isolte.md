The tallest mountain in the [[Cthonic Remains]] realm of [[Mag Duinn]], Spion Isolte, is situated in the heart of the [[Upper Plateau]] region of [[Ard Breahnhelle]]. Due to its climate and geography, it has historically been considered uninhabitable. Despite the challenges, [[Afonso Roche]], a famed knight who once served Duque [[Ronaldo Sintaflores]], seeks to establish his seat of power on this spire of rock.

# Godslayer

Though the mountain has long been a fixture of religious iconography and folkloric story, it became truly infamous when the [[Primordial Gods|Primordial]] [[Nazerack]] impaled himself on the peak of the mountain as he burrowed into the realm from the surface. The massive body of the God put the crest of Spion Isolte through its head the way one might put a head on a pike. Though it's tremendous body coiled in the Vale de Raiz below, it met its death via the mountain. Since then, the mountain has become infamous among the denizens of Mag Duinn, who fear its power, yet praise it's mercy for halting the rampage of the sky God.

# Settlement

Shortly after the dawn of the calamity, a knight presented himself to Duque Ronaldo's court, claiming to have come from Spion Isolte. He pledged his sword to the Lord and spent a long career in service to the province. In his will, Ronaldo left custody of the mountain to his most trusted knight - Afonso - who often talked about restoring the glory of his home in its heights.

Since then, Afonso has withdrawn from society and has amassed a mighty fortress, as well as a small army of fiends and curs who are singularly dedicated to his vision. He calls this settlement [[Novo Ayodar]], which he claims is a reflection of a castle from a higher plain. Few who visit this castle return without wearing the colors of his army.
