The Upper Plateau constitutes the eastern and western edges of the [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] realm of [[Mag Duinn]]. Generally, the Upper Plateau flanks the lower part of the region, the [[Vale de Raiz]], though there is quite a bit of elevation variation between the different provinces.

# Provinces

There are seven major provinces in the Upper Plateau:

- [[Ard Breahnhelle]]: boasting the highest elevation in the land, Breahnhelle has historically been ruled by the Sintaflores bloodline.
- [[Dubh Sliogán]]: The sacred woodlands, said to be the home of the Goddess [[Nespera]].
- [[Foghladha Highlands]]: An enchanted woodland that sits directly above the Vale de Raiz.
- [[Folgosinho]]: Rich in natural resources, this province has historically been used as a mining outpost.
- [[Pico Lyanor]]: Former home to a noble queen who met a tragic end, now serves as an important pilgrimage site.
- [[Prazeres]]: Once one of the most joyful provinces in Mag Duinn, Prazers has become little more than a sprawling graveyard.
- [[Scathanna]]: The most secluded province in the realm, Scathanna is the capital of the realm.
