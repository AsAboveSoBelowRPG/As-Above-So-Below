In the realm of Mag Duinn, there is no more famed mortal sorcerer than Pedro Pipocas, though recent centuries of seclusion have tempered his legend to the point where most denizens of the [[Vale de Raiz]] suspect him of being little more than a fictional character invented as the protagonist of fantastical tales.

# Early Life

Stories of Pipocas seem to precede the arrival of the [[Rustic God]] [[Nespera]], with early accounts saying the sorcerer was the first to welcome the Goddess when she awakened. Ancient literature from the dawn of the current civilization seem to suggest that Pipocas was alive during the time of the [[Primordial Gods|Primordial]] [[Aodannach]], the creeping miasma that choked the realm and suffocated any life that attempted to take purchase. In these stories, he is either presented as Her lover or Her eternal rival that finally vanquished the deity, paving the way for Nespera's rise.

# Heroic Deeds

During his youth, Pipocas acted as a hero, adventuring with other kind-hearted warriors to defend the fledgling culture during its infancy when the threats were numerous. During this time he became a beloved figure and was often called the Father of Mag Duinn.

These selfless acts earned the admiration of Nespera, who offered the mage a seat by her side to rule the lands - an offer he turned down, instead encouraging the young goddess to, instead, elect a committee of mortals to rule the very lands they occupied. Together, the two of them scoured the land to assemble the [[Snáithe Airgid]].

During the insurrection by the insidious [[Feall Sisters]] who scattered the Airgid in favor of their own sisterhood, the [[Dramhaíl Coven]], Pipocas did not interfere. In his infamous address to the people of [[Aldeia de Raiz]] after the dissolution of the Airgid, he warned that the people of the realm were entering a time of hardship that would only be overcome by a vulcanization of the people, and that, by bailing them out he was ultimately weakening their defenses. This was the end of the period of growth and abundance in the realm and the beginning of the dark times that have come to define the Cthonic pocket.

# Rumors

Contemporaneous rumors suggest that Pipocas and the youngest [[Feall Sisters|Feall Sister]] [[Easóg]] were romantically involved. For centuries the two lived together on the hill above Raiz - a number of these accounts suggesting the pair even raised a child together. Shortly before the insurrection, Pipocas disappeared. It was during this time that an opportunistic magnate moved onto the hill, knocking down the witch's cromlech and building his [[The De Cabra Murders|cursed estate]] in its place. Pipocas and Easóg never saw each other again.

# Retirement

Those who still know the name Pedro Pipocas treat the ancient mage with the respect due to a god. Among those are the scholars of the valley and the nobles of the [[Upper Plateau]]. Long time patrons of his were the [[Ronaldo Sintaflores|Duque]] and [[Catarina Sintaflores|Duquesa]] of [[Ard Breahnhelle]], where the sorcerer was long known to spend considerable time. He retains a permanent apartment in the [[Casa da Lua Culpada]], though he has not employed it in ages. He has even stopped attending [[The Lament Masquerade]] - an event protected by the powerful spells he concocted at the behest of Duque Ronaldo.
