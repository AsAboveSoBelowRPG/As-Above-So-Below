Once a year nobles from across [[Mag Duinn]] gather for a grand party with the goal of raising money for the [[Sanatório Desesperança]], which offers its services free of charge to the unfortunate residents of the [[Vale de Raiz]]. Though the party began as a humanitarian event, it has become one of the most prestigious society balls in the realm, and, though everyone is masked, it is considered uncouth to not have a story to tell of your time at the Masquerade afterwards. Purchasing entry costs 1,500 gold, unless you have a writ of invitation.

# Rules

While the event is known for its free and debaucherous atmosphere where attendees can indulge in any number of sinful delights, those who join the masquerade agree to abide by a few strict rules of conduct during the night. Failing to abide by these guidelines can have disastrous results, as the event is protected by a spell set by the famed sorcerer [[Pedro Pipocas]].

#### 1. Faces must always be obscured 
All other clothing is considered optional during the masquerade, but, for your own protection, masks must be worn at all times. 

#### 2. Identity is sacred; Identity is a weapon
On a night where rich nobles gather to indulge in their darkest pleasures, remaining anonymous is imperative. In a palace where money is a trivial commodity, your identity is the only asset that matters. Being named not only brings great shame, but puts you at the mercy of your accuser.

#### 3. Use names only in self defense
You may recognize a reveler: they could be friend or foe. The Masquerade is a night to set aside societal connections and enjoy an unfettered celebration. While casting a name is not forbidden, it should only be done under the most dire of circumstances. A word of warning: false identification has dire consequences.

#### 4. The celebration persists
At midnight, the lift will lock for 24 hours. During that time, the Masquerade will continue unimpeded - nobody leaves, nobody joins. Try to avoid incurring any fatal injuries, and, if possible, giving fatal injuries to others. If you become a burden to the party, you will be ejected, and the trip down is much faster without a lift.

#### 5. A minha casa é a tua casa
Enjoy all the amenities of the [[Casa da Lua Culpada]]: everything from the sprawling verandas, the endless pantry and kitchen, the guest chambers and the bath house are all available to you. The only area that remains off limits are the royal apartments on the top floor of the palace.

# Events and Attractions

- **Music and Dancing**: Famed musician [[Apelonia Gritador]] will bring her band to entertain in the grand ballroom. They will perform during the entirety of the masquerade, so find a partner and be sure to hit the dance floor.
- **Endless Buffet:** You've never seen as much food as this! The fully manned kitchen will be serving fresh food day and night. Find a seat in the grand dining hall and a server will see that you eat and drink exactly what you desire.
- **Partner Bazaar:** Put yourself on the block, or spend a few coins to buy yourself a companion for an hour. The bazaar is the perfect way to break the ice with someone who has caught your eye.
- **Exotic Animal Menagerie:** Courtesy of [[Afonso Roche]] and his Beastwardens, visit the western veranda to see strange creatures live in captivity.
- **Rare Goods Auction:** Spend some extra coin to bid on these priceless treasures. Dig deep: remember, the money goes to a charitable cause! You're not likely to find precious and strange items like these elsewhere in the realm.
- **The Fool's Court:** The Duquesa relinquishes her rule in this symbolic ritual in which her throne sits vacant for any to claim. See for yourself if the head that wears the crown truly is heavy.
- **Hourly Prayer Service:** Priestess [[Irmã Tempestade]] will be available in the temple to lead the devout in prayer, for those who prefer a more pious experience at the Masquerade. If you haven't heard the good news, stop in and convert to the new religion, [[The Groundskeepers]], at your convenience.

# Amenities

Be sure to indulge in any of the amenities the [[Casa da Lua Culpada]] has to offer. Guest suites are available on a first come, first serve basis (hourly visits are acceptable). The kitchen and bar staff are equipped to satisfy any desire. The vast verandas and the spring garden offer a quiet respite to the endless joy of the festival, as does the grand bathhouse on the second floor.
