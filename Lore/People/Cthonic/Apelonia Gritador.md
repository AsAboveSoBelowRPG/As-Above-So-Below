Though she is rather young, Apelonia has made a tremendous impact on the [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] realm of Mag Duinn as a travelling cellist - earning acclaim both in the [[Vale de Raiz]] as well as the [[Upper Plateau]] where she has been invited to play in high profile venues such as the capital of [[Scathanna]] and as a repeat band leader during [[The Lament Masquerade]] in [[Ard Breahnhelle]].

# Personal Life

Being born in Southwest Mag Duinn, Apelonia desired for more than the small village of her birth. Finding that her natural penchant for making music offered her an excuse and the means to travel, she threw herself at the mastery of her preferred instrument - the cello. Embarking on her own, she first gained a reputation playing at the gambling halls and taverns in the [[Slíomach Bog]] before expanding eastward to the villages of [[Aldeia de Raiz]] and [[Aldeia de Espiga]], where she befriended the [[Família do Corvinho]] - specifically the middle daughter Rosa.

Acting as her patron, the Corvinho's helped her find an audience in the [[Upper Plateau]], playing in [[Folgosinho]] and, eventually, [[Ard Breahnhelle]] where she became fast friends with the [[Catarina Sintaflores|Duquesa]] and her daughter. Through this connection she was able to establish a foothold amongst the nobles and, within a year, became the most desired act amongst the socialites of the realm.

# Personality

Spending her early years playing the raucous and dangerous dens in [[Aldeia de Alagadiço]] primed her to be a tough woman who doesn't accept nonsense and can handle herself as easily in a [[Navigator]] camp as she can in a formal ballroom. Her mischievous smile and quick wit make her a compelling and endearing woman - something that has helped her fame grow exponentially in such a short time.

Though she suffered a great loss when her best friend, Rosa Corvinho, died, she still managed to pull it together and find the strength to play the annual masquerade - largely thanks to the escort provided by [[The Bastards of Light]], who helped her escape the cursed village of Espiga. In an attempt to repay the debt, she helped [[Kadav Papazuzian]] sneak into the Masquerade as her clarinet player.
