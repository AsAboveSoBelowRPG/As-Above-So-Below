The enigmatic High Priest in service to [[The Mycelial Thread]], Neburchadrezzar has single-handedly shaped the social and political landscape of [[Aldeia de Espiga]] for over 50 years. Though he has been deeply calculating and cautious, planning every move to the most minute detail, in his thirst for ultimate destruction he abandoned his ruse as he seeks utter destruction of the village he was born into.

# Identity

Through his entire career both ascending through the ranks of the cult and his eventual leadership of it, none other than the neighboring High Priest of [[Aldeia de Raiz]], [[Ataroth]], knew his identity.

He was finally exposed due to a journal left behind when the High Priest abandoned the village to seek it's ultimate destruction - the tome outing him as the influential patriarch of the [[Família do Corvinho]] - Gustavo Corvinho. By the time this was discovered it was too late to stop him from setting the machinations of destruction into motion.
