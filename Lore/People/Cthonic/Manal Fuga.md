Manal is a proud and humble man that has spent his entire life working in the Ventodados Granary for the late [[Família do Corvinho|Gustavo Corvinho]]. After his employer vanished and was presumed deceased, Claudia left management to her vindictive daughter [[Graça Corvinho]], who actively sought to run the business into the ground. Despite the abuse he suffered at her hands, Manal cared deeply about his profession and the workers at the granary. By the time of the[[Beast in the East|fateful showdown]] between [[The Bastards of Light]] and [[Pica Pau]], he was the only remaining employee.

Though the blight left most citizens of Espiga hopeless and broken, this was a new chapter for Manal. The new owner of the Ventodados Granary, [[Errndar Bearfinder]], put Manal in charge of the mill, raising him to a position he had long aspired to obtain. With the citizens of the village working tirelessly to rebuild, Manal has put his entire life into restoring the mill to its former glory.
