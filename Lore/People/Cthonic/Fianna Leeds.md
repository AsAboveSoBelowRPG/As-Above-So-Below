Fianna is the only daughter of a prominent [[Upper Plateau]] noble Maria Leeds. Maria, a minor noble, wanted to elevate her position in the spires and decided an act of intimidation would set her apart from the other families. She embarked on a quest to tame a wretched hill giant that stalked the province named [[Greganio]]. Slipping into the creature's den, the woman dominated the enormous, deformed creature - ultimately forcing the hideous creature to mate with her. Maria knew the others wouldn't believe her story, but giving birth to a giant child would be the ultimate proof.

The fetus grew to such a tremendous size in her womb that she was forced to lay on her side for most of the pregnancy, and the child was so enormous that it had to be cut from her belly. Unfortunately, she died to deliver her baby.

Fianna was raised by the staff of her mother's estate, which she inherited. She grew to be an incredibly educated and sharp woman, but crass and violent - always finding ways to undercut the societal norms. As such, she is equally alluring and reviled by the nobles of Breahnhelle. Those who find her charms infectious are made to compete for her affections, often by sparring with each other until only one is left standing. This fighting is seen as entirely boorish and low class, but, like her mother, she wishes to display her dominance by belittling those around her.

She very rarely socializes with others outside of her own estate, though she loves attending [[The Lament Masquerade]] - both because she has a mild obsession with the bath house in the [[Casa da Lua Culpada]], and because she knows that [[Catarina Sintaflores]] despises her, and it is the one night a year she can't refuse her entrance. Even though her unusual size makes her stand out, no one is brave enough to expose her during the event. Rather, people have learned to stay clear of the bath house during the festival, lest they get drawn into her fighting pit.
