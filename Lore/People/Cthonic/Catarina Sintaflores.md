Duquesa Catarina is the widowed ruler of the [[Mag Duinn]] province of [[Ard Breahnhelle]]. Though she was born to the noble Al-Altiala family and married the most powerful man in her region of the [[Cthonic Remains]], she has always remained a grounded and compassionate person. As such, she is deeply adored by her people, and by the other nobles of the [[Upper Plateau]].

# Early Life

Catarina was born to Sofia and Armand Al-Atiala in the noble spires of Breahnhelle, but sent to the capital in [[Scathanna]] to be educated at a renowned finishing school. It was at a society ball here that she met her future husband [[Ronaldo Sintaflores]], as well as her best friend [[Isabel Salinas]] - an older socialite who runs the [[Sanatório Desesperança]]. 

After a brief courtship, arrangements were made between her father and Ronaldo for the two to wed, and she returned to her home province to rule alongside her beloved. For decades the two lived in bliss until Ronaldo was struck by a mysterious illness. Despite the best efforts of her friend Isabel, who put the hospital's greatest physician - [[Sylcan Calcador]] - on the case, he succumbed to the disease and left her widowed.

The entire province grieved for a year, marking the only time since it's inception that the fundraising masquerade didn't take place, though it was this very revelry that pulled her and the other nobles out of their depression. Since then, Duquesa Catarina has ruled alone, with her oldest son [[Rhagorol Sintaflores]] as her advisor.

# Tensions in Breahnhelle

In his will, Ronaldo left the land around the great mountain [[Spion Isolte]] to his close friend and dedicated knight [[Afonso Roche]]. Thinking little of it, Catarina happily arranged for her deceased husband's wishes to be actualized.

Within a decade, however, the former loyalist had established a stronghold and began pulling political sway towards his potential leadership, under the banner of his new castle [[Novo Ayodar]]. While Roche has made no attempt to siege her home, [[Casa da Lua Culpada]], there is a deep tension between the two houses.

Each year, the two unofficially measure their support amongst the noble houses of the province by surveying the colors and costumes of those who attend the yearly [[The Lament Masquerade|Masquerade]] held as a benefit for Isabel's hospital in the [[Vale de Raiz]].
