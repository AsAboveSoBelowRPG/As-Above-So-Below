Ronaldo Sintaflores, the 24th Duque of [[Ard Breahnhelle]], was known to be a just and kind ruler. As reagent of the [[Upper Plateau]]'s harshest province, Ronaldo routinely had to make difficult choices for the sake of his people. As such, he was never one to take gains while others suffered, and was the first to make sacrifices when the modest harvest wasn't enough to feed the realm.

At the age of 38 Ronaldo married a Breahnhelle noble named [[Catarina Sintaflores|Catarina]], and the two of them enjoyed a peaceful and gentle reign until he passed away. By his side, always, was his friend and most dedicated knight, [[Afonso Roche]], who claimed to hail from the tallest mountain in the realm, [[Spion Isolte]].

# Life and Legacy

Ronaldo was a caring man and beloved by both his people and his family. He had three children with his wife Catarina, two boys and one girl. The youngest son and his daughter relocated to the capital in [[Scathanna]] by invitation of Lord [[Salvadaro Harbin Glumbo]].

Despite the seclusion and difficulty of travel both to and within his province, his warm hospitality made Breahnhelle a hotspot for realm nobles, many of which built second homes in the lofty spires surrounding his palace, [[Casa da Lua Culpada]]. 

Through a friendship with [[Isabel Salinas]], the proprietress of the [[Sanatório Desesperança]], the Duque and Duquesa leveraged their wealthy noble friends to start an annual fundraiser for the hospital on the [[Ilha Lamento]] in the [[Vale de Raiz]]. This annual party, [[The Lament Masquerade]], is held at great expense in their home. It draws wealthy nobles from across the whole of [[Mag Duinn]] - including the Lord and Lady of the realm. This party has continued since his passing.

When the Duque fell ill with a strange and malicious disease, Isabel brought her finest physician, [[Sylcan Calcador]], to personally treat the ailing ruler. Despite their best attempts to keep the disease at bay, ultimately he succumbed to the mysterious illness. That year there was no Masquerade, and his funeral took over a month, as those who wished to pay their respects kept arriving. He is interred in the royal plot in [[Cemitério dos Prazeres]].

In his will, he left custody of [[Spion Isolte]] to his most trusted knight, and the only man he ever met who claimed to hail from the lifeless spire - Afonso.
