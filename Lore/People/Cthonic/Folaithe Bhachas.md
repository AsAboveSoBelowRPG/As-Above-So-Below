Born into a noble family of the [[Upper Plateau]], Folaithe and his older brother [[Blantól Bhachas]] were inseparable until a woman came between them. The two feuded publicly over the attention of [[Trua Lámh]], who ultimately chose the younger Bhachas brother as her consort. This lead to a public spectacle when Folaithe was forced to expose his brother during [[The Lament Masquerade]] to suppress his rage. 

Though he was crushed to lose his brother, he refused to let it impede his zest for life and continues to live a decadent life with his partner. While the revelers at the Masquerade view Blantól's antics as the amusing actions of a madman, there is a tinge of sadness about how he has lost himself to his mental illness.
