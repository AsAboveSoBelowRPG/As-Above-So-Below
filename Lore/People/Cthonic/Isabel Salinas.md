Taking inspiration from the timeless healers of the past, Isabel Salinas made it her life's work to bring medicine to the suffering in the [[Vale de Raiz]]. Setting up her practice in an abandoned clinic on [[Ilha Lamento]], she managed to grow the scope of her outreach into the massive [[Sanatório Desesperança]]. Having collected the finest healers in the realm into a single building, she has become known, far and wide, as the savior of the sick.

# Early Life and Practice

Not much is known about where Isabel came from or what she did during her youth; when asked she claims that she was always a healer, and that she did so answering a call from the Goddess [[Nespera]]. She first gained notoriety when she arrived in [[Scathanna]] seeking support from the nobles to aid her humanitarian efforts. The people of the capital were so fetched by her compassion and beauty that she became a fixture among the upper class.

During her time campaigning for funds and donations in the capital, she met a number of people that would change the course of her life. First was the young daughter of a small noble family from [[Ard Breahnhelle]], [[Catarina Sintaflores]], who would later become the duquesa of the province - as well as Isabel's best friend and life-long benefactor. This woman would later establish [[The Lament Masquerade]] as a way to provide steady funds for the hospital.

The second fateful encounter was with [[Afonso Roche]] who, at the time, served as [[Ronaldo Sintaflores]] personal guard. The two fell madly in love and, in the [[Casa da Lua Culpada]], married by oath of [[The Ashen Bough|the old ways]]. To the delight of her best friend, an apartment was made for the two in the noble manor - these were the brightest days of Isabel's life.

# The Descent

When Duque Ronaldo died unexpectedly of a disease she failed to heal - despite the aid of her most accomplished physician, [[Sylcan Calcador]], Isabel began to fall into a deep depression. This sadness only compounded when her husband Afonso moved them from the manor in Breahnhelle into the snowy wastes of [[Novo Ayodar]] where he was establishing his own stronghold.

During this time there was a great change in Afonso's character. The chivalrous knight with an unshakable sense of justice and dedication became moody and dark after he excavated something from the ruins on [[Spion Isolte]]. When their arguments turned physical, Isabel fled the [[Upper Plateau]] to seek shelter in her [[Sanatório Desesperança]]. Though [[Catarina Sintaflores|Catarina]] begged her to, instead, return to the [[Casa da Lua Culpada]] and reside with her, she claimed she couldn't remain in the same province as Afonso and fled - only returning once a year for the Masquerade.

# Seclusion

Mistress Isabel has since turned the Sanatório into her own personal fortress. While it still accepts the suffering of [[Mag Duinn]], those who return from their treatments may be cured of what sent them to seek help, but are not exactly what one might call sentient or healthy. As these wretched souls start to flood the banks of [[Ilha Lamento]], people are starting to question what Mistress Isabel is doing in behind those locked doors.
