Trua is the daughter of a lesser noble from the spires of Breahnhelle in the [[Upper Plateau]]. The Láhm family didn't accomplish much of note until their stunning daughter went to the court of the [[Ronaldo Sintaflores|Duque]] and [[Catarina Sintaflores|Duquesa]]. She made her debut at [[The Lament Masquerade]] where two prominent brothers - [[Blantól Bhachas]] and [[Folaithe Bhachas]] - both fell madly in love with her. Though the two competed for her affections, she ultimately chose the younger of the two, seemingly driving the elder to madness.

Since the two officially formed a union, Blantól has accosted the couple every year at the Masquerade, calling Folaithe by strange and unusual names despite knowing his brother's name. The attention this dramatic love triangle garnered, primarily from the annual public spectacle, has turned Trua into one of the most enigmatic and desired woman at the masquerade. 

Despite being a fixture of socialite events for decades, few have so much as heard her voice or seen her unmasked. Rumors say that her beauty is too stunning to see unmasked, and that she hides her identity as to not insight a violent or riotous incident among the upper nobles. In spite of her vexing assets, Trua has never attended the grand courts of the capital in [[Scathanna]], and has reportedly turned down a personal invitation from Lord [[Salvadaro Harbin Glumbo]] to visit his palace.

She seems rather content to remain in Breahnhelle with her lover, enjoying the blessed life they share in the spires.
