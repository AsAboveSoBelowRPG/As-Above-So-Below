While decorum at [[The Lament Masquerade]] is considered with greater regard than the nobles hold for the laws of the province, Blantól repeatedly ignores these regulations at the expense of his own health. Each year he accosts his brother [[Folaithe Bhachas]], and, as such, is subject to the protective curse placed over the event by the sorcerer [[Pedro Pipocas]].

Despite violating the laws of the masquerade, his outbursts have become a fixture of the event, with many feeling the celebration doesn't officially begin until Blantól makes his accusation. His antics have become a beloved feature of the festivities, though he seems to be taking this very seriously.

# The Falling Out

Blantól and Folaithe were the only sons of a wealthy lord in the priviledged spires of [[Ard Breahnhelle]], and grew up as inseparable companions. The two did everything together, and you would rarely see one without the other looming like a shadow nearby. When their father died, the two evenly split the inheritance, overcoming the most perilous pitfall facing the wealthy inhabitants of the [[Upper Plateau]].

That was all to change when they encountered a beautiful young woman named [[Trua Lámh]]. Both brothers fell madly in love with the woman and began campaigns of love seeking to court her. This romantic rivalry drove a wedge between the two brothers, inevitably destroying their relationship when Blantól witnessed the two of them engaging their passions at the Lament Masquerade. When Blantól made a scene, Folaithe evoked the laws of the party to gain control of his aggressive sibling by exposing his identity.

This was the last time the brothers interacted for an entire decade; Blantól isolated himself from society, most assuming it was a result of the shame he endured at the masquerade. When he finally returned to the celebration, he began his campaign of attempting to unmask his brother - but vexingly, rather than calling him by his known name, began accusing him by strange names that seemed more befitting of beasts than man.

# Demonology

While Blantól was deeply shamed by his behavior at the masquerade and for the petty rivalry with his brother in general, it wasn't the root of his isolation.

A week after the confrontation, Blantól called upon his brother to apologize and give his blessing to the two lovers. However, when he spoke to his brother he knew that something of him was off. He looked the same, had the same voice, the same kind eyes - but there was something missing, something only someone who spent every day of their lives together would notice. 

Blantól became convinced that his brother had been replaced by a demonic imposter, and rededicated his life to uncovering the identity of the fiend that had replaced him. Over the decade that he was in isolation, Blantól was actually studying grimoires and codex, researching prominent demonic entities of the [[Cthonic Remains]]. 

Knowing that Pipocas's spell at the Masquerade would grant him control over the entity, he used it as the staging point for his ambush. After ten years he was certain he had pinpointed the identity of the doppelganger, and arrived at [[Casa da Lua Culpada]] to confront Folaithe. Unfortunately, he didn't name the correct entity and suffered the repercussions of the curse that governs misidentifications at the ball. Each year he would return to his studies and return the next year with another suspected demonic name.

# The Curse

By the year AC'200, Blantól's body had been mostly consumed by the curse. Years of false accusations had left him mostly paralyzed and nearly entirely wooden. Knowing it was his last chance to name the demon, he poured over his notes and came to the conclusion that Bleblanatanblant was the only sensible demon he had yet to name. When it failed, he was crushed and sulked in the ballroom, looking over his research.

Much to his surprise, he found himself talking to a strange reveler named [[Kadav Papazuzian]] - his decades of research into demonology immediately betrayed something about the being. He didn't match physical descriptions of the famed lower Cthonic Archdemon, Blantól was certain that Kadav was the lost [[Pazu Pazuzu]] who vanished from the lower realm after a pact made with a surface sorcerer. He resisted the urge to expose the demon publicly, but expressed his suspicions in private.
