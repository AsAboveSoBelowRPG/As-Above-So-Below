While much of the lives of these infamous triplets remain a mystery, their impact on the [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] realm of Mag Duinn is immeasurable. Most of what we know about their lives before joining the Coven of the Goddess finds roots in myth and rumor, though their lives continue to be a source of study and fascination.

# Early Life

There are no records of triplets born around the time the Feall were discovered, so pragmatic scholars insist they must have been born of reclusive denizens in the nearby woodlands. The first mention of the children surfaces in the ledgers of a merchant from [[Aldeia de Alagadiço]] who notes that he found three orphans with golden hair and supernatural beauty abandoned at the Trasbhealach crossroads. In the account, the merchant stopped to inquire as to their situation, asking if they had parents, if they were in need of trouble, or a ride. According to his account, the three sisters stared at him silently without hope or fear, and it was only when he attempted to approach them that they scattered into the woods.

In his account, he notes that this was a horrible omen that foretold the coming of disastrous times. This encounter has survived these many years because the tale spread across the land - a fable that would have likely been dismissed as superstition or lie, had his warning not come to pass.

# Prophecy

The people of Mag Duinn had reason to fear this report, as it aligned with an early prophecy delivered by the Goddess [[Zell]] when she first visited the realm. Though she wrote many such predictions through the ages, this ominous quatrain has commanded a fascination through the ages - a fear made real by the merchant's sighting. The prophecy reads as such:

> By the blood of the unknowable ancients, the stones that witnessed the shaping of the realm will give birth to the children of undoing. On six legs, She will walk the land sowing only misfortune for those who witness them.
> 
> Only when a foreign light penetrates the darkness shall their reign be broken; and only through a wretched sacrifice shall they find, again, their divinity. The land will find salvation in the luminous beings from beyond, or destruction from the waking of an impenetrable darkness.

# Witches of the Crossroads

As the years went on, the triplets went from a symbol of ancient fear to a mild curiosity. They devoted themselves to the study of profane and forbidden magics, making the crossroads of Trasbhealach their home. In due time, once the locals realized they had no desire to sow the destruction envisioned by the prophetess, desperate people began to seek the sisters out for their wicked gifts.

During these centuries, the three became known for their stunning beauty and their horrifying methods - always delivering what was asked of them, but at costs unfathomable to those who came to seek their aid. The three sisters, though they dwelled together in the house at the crossroads, often embarked for decades at a time to chase some adventure alone; though they always found themselves back together again.

Sensing their inherent power, the holy coven of the [[Snáithe Airgid]] offered the three positions on the council that ruled the realm by the grace of [[Nespera]]. The members of the Airgid believed that there were grounds for concern in Zell's prophecy, but thought that, perhaps, with the guidance of the coven, they might subvert a calamity and turn them into allies of the realm.

# Insurrection

Though the sisters took part in the Goddesses Coven, this ultimately didn't suit them. Shortly before the coming of [[Nazerack]], the three staged an insurrection against the coven, scattering the members into hiding. In its place, the three formed a wicked sisterhood bent on corrupting the realm and bringing it to ruin - just as predicted. They named their partnership the [[Dramhaíl Coven]], and have brazenly wrought destruction and strife for those who live in the Valley of Roots. None of their former coven mates have stood against them, and the new Lord of the Land, [[Salvadaro Harbin Glumbo]], hardly seems to take notice of their oppression of the lower realm.
