Hailing from an era before the Grimpa became slaves to the tyrants of the [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] realm of Mag Duinn, Afonydd left his home in the mountains to seek a living on the open waters of the valley. During this time, Afonydd gained a reputation as the Savior of the Sea - more frequently using his net to pull drowning souls from the waters than fish. Though he didn't net much in the way of supper, those he saved never allowed him to go hungry.

# Snáithe Airgid

Afonydd was the first elected member of the Goddess [[Nespera]]'s coven, [[Snáithe Airgid]], as well as the first male to enter the sisterhood. He was nominated for his compassion and service. The vote was unanimous in support for his election, and when offered the opportunity to serve the realm, he didn't hesitate for a moment.

Nespera gifted Afonydd her tibia, claiming it represented his stature which was incongruous with his height.

Agonydd chose the great expanse of [[Loch Linne]] as his domain. It was there he remained after the dread [[Feall Sisters]] staged their insurrection, as the loch is a dangerous realm not easily navigated. He remained there until the coming of [[Nazerack]] brought the calamity to the realm. Shortly thereafter he vanished from the waves.
