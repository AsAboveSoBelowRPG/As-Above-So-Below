Alcochete is known as the First Sorceress of Mag Duinn; the being who brought magic to the realm. Little is known of her origin or life before she joined the [[Snáithe Airgid]], but her short life was one of legend and intrigue.

# Snáithe Airgid

Alcochete was the first member chosen by the goddess [[Nespera]] to join the holy coven. Though the Snáithe had no official leader, it was Alcochete's voice that carried the most weight when it came to coven policy and decisions about how the realm should be governed. Despite her sway within the sisterhood, she rarely ever spoke unless she was called upon to do so, and lived a quiet life of solitude.

Unlike other members of the Snáithe, Alcochete chose no region to govern; opting instead to drift across the realm in the disguise of a beggar, seeking to intrude on the hospitality of those she represented in the coven. Those who met her, clothed in filthy rags and smelling of filth, and provided her whatever modest means they could afford were blessed with the gift of magic. Those who turned her away came to ruin.

When the Goddess blessed the coven with her body, she entrusted Alcochete with her tongue, symbolically stating that she was the only one who could speak for Nespera.

# Rumors and Origin

As the most enigmatic member of the coven, little can be said for certain about her background - though that hardly kept the people of the realm from speculating.

Since there are no records of her life before the establishment of the Snáithe, and since her skin tone bears more than a passing resemblance to depictions of Nespera, it is often put forth that Alcochete is the daughter of the Goddess, or, even sometimes, that she is the Goddess in disguise. 

Other rumors say that she came to the realm from the [[Surface Layer]], or perhaps even from the [[Realm of the Sky]]. On the other hand, there are also tales that she came from a deeper pocket of the [[Cthonic Remains]] - some even say she potentially came all the way from [[Tehom]], the seat of the underworld. None of this has ever been corroborated, and can easily be dismissed as groundless gossip.

# Life After the Snáithe

When the Coven was dismantled by the treacherous [[Feall Sisters]], it appears that she vanished from record. No accounts list her as having been slain during the insurrection, but there has also never been another sighting of her in the realm since.

Many who worship [[The Ashen Bough]] still set up small altars to Alcochete and pray for her return to the land, so that she might, once more, give voice to the Goddess' will.
