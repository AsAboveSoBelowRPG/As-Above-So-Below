Though the [[Feall Sisters]] are triplets of the same age, Easóg was the last of the three to come into being, and, as such, carried a deep attachment to her sisters [[Colma]] and [[Fíonnuar]] from the moment she was born. Together the three of them joined the fabled [[Snáithe Airgid]], and together they destroyed it - seeking, instead, to rule with their [[Dramhaíl Coven]]. 

# Personality

From an early age, Easóg struggled with separation anxiety and fears of abandonment, clinging to her sisters sides at all times. When the [[Rustic God]] [[Nespera]] offered them positions on the [[Snáithe Airgid]], she accepted only because Colma insisted, not because she had a desire to rule. When it came time to choose domains, she opted to maintain the small garden she had been cultivating into a place of elemental power on the hill overlooking [[Aldeia de Raiz]]. 

When Nespera carved her body to give each of the members a piece of her, Easóg was given Her teeth, as the goddess felt she needed some fangs. Easóg was very clearly the gentlest of the sisters, and also the most impressionable, so when Colma began plotting to overthrow the coven, she wasn't difficult to manipulate, though she was also the most remorseful of what she had done.

# Consort

It was no secret that she and the heroic sorcerer [[Pedro Pipocas]] were involved in a long-term relationship. The two cohabitated together for dozens of years in her home above the village. There was no doubt about the authenticity of their love, though Pipocas could not reconcile with her betrayal of the Airgid and abandoned her after the insurrection.

# Corruption

Shortly after Pipocas left, a wealthy noble from [[Ard Breahnhelle]] named [[The De Cabra Murders|Francisco D'Albergaria]] relocated to the [[Vale de Raiz]] and destroyed her cromlech to build his manor. The loss of her treasured home paired with the abandonment by her true love and the remorse she felt over betraying her friends in the Airgid twisted Easóg into a beastly hag and she adopted a new name to reflect her rebirth as a monster: [[Theda Vidigueira]].

Ultimately, Easóg died that day, though Theda was vanquished by a group of unlikely heroes known as [[The Bastards of Light]] who arrived at her manor to dine with [[Salvadaro Harbin Glumbo]] and [[Magdalena Isabel A'Dora Glumbo]] who she had planned to sacrifice the night the group was meant to join the couple for dinner.

Only Pipocas mourned her death, weeping for the woman he loved, not the monster she had become.
