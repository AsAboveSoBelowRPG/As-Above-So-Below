Heralded as the most beautiful mortal woman in all of the [[Cthonic Remains]], Vagar learned to fear time from a young age - forever facing the inevitability of losing her youth and beauty. Spurred on by her selfish desires, she channeled this fear into a desire to defeat disease and halt her aging, so she might live forever in her beauty.

As she developed tinctures, salves and potions meant to forestall the march of time, she found that these medicines held value beyond vanity, and that they were able to cure the maladies of those around her. Though she never abandoned her pursuit of eternal youth, she found she actually preferred the attention of those who appreciated her gifts of healing rather than those who just admired her form and figure.

# Snáithe Airgid

Vagar was elected to the holy coven of the [[Snáithe Airgid]] after [[Alcochete]], in her disguise as a filthy beggar, was treated as a guest of honor when she knocked on the healer's door. She brought the ragged woman inside and treated her rashes, bites and maladies without fear of contamination. When Alcochete brought her name before the other members of the coven, her endorsement was enough to convince the others of her worthiness. She is the only member nominated by Alcochete.

When [[Nespera]] gave of herself to those who sat on the council, she gifted the beautiful woman her ear, in hopes that she would always hear the plight of those in the realm who suffered. So inspired by this gift, Vagar took [[Ilha Lamento]] as her domain and established a small clinic where the [[Sanatório Desesperança]] now stands.

Her companion on the coven, [[Afonydd]], protected the island after the [[Feall Sisters]] staged their coup and sought to eliminate the other members. Regardless, her fate was lost to history, as she ultimately vanished sometime around the calamity.
