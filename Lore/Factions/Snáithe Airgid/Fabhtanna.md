Fabhtanna is the second of the [[Feall Sisters]], and the most antisocial of the triplets. Together with her cursed siblings, they formed the [[Dramhaíl Coven]] from the ashes of the [[Snáithe Airgid]]. Since the dawning of the calamity in their [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] realm, she has receded from public view, retreating to her cavernous lair in the mountains.

# Snáithe Airgid

Though she personally objected to joining the coven assembled by the Goddess [[Nespera]], Fabhtanna was convinced by her sister [[Colma]], who insisted it would be the safest place for them in a world full of danger. Knowing that the three must come as a package, and that they would not be admitted individually, she relented and agreed to join the Airgid. During her time in the coven, she never knew happiness, and often found the dynamics of belonging to a group to be overwhelming.

To find a semblance of quiet solitude, Fabhtanna chose the subterranean caverns of the realm as her domain, opting to explore the ancient dark mines alone rather than engaging in realm politics with her sisters.

When Nespera made gifts of her body, she trusted her intestines to Fabhy - saying that the young witch should always trust her gut instincts; adding with a wry smile that Fabhy spent so much of her time in the bowels of the earth that she ought to carry those of the Goddess as well.

# Dramhaíl Insurrection

Colma, who felt the Feall Sisters should carry greater authority within the coven, began to hatch a plot to dismantle the Airgid and replace it with a new sisterhood composed only of the triplets. Fabhy, who never particularly cared for the organization, was happy to remove herself from the social obligations of the coven, whereas [[Easóg]] required more convincing.

Ultimately the three staged an uprising against the holy coven, scattering its former members across the realm. However, the power they sought to attain never fell into place, as, once the structure of the Airgid was dismantled the trio began infighting, and waged an internal war against each other - each vying for power over the others.

Since the calamity fell upon Mag Duinn, the sisters seemed to have ceased their war, quietly retreating to their domains and leaving the land, for the first time in generations, in relative peace.
