Wildly popular with the early cultures of the realm, Adaiwen was a born performer who traveled the land spreading cheer to villages that were particularly challenged, especially small agricultural towns in the valley. Moreso, Adaiwen was never shy about pitching in for harvest or planting, insisting that he was born in similar circumstances - though his actual birthplace is unknown.

Adaiwen never settled in one place for more than a week, insisting that there was always another town that needed his attention somewhere down the road. Because he was able to visit so many different villages, his fame was unrivalled, and when the goddess [[Nespera]] put together her sacred coven, the [[Snáithe Airgid]], he was invited to join as the spirit of the valley.

When the Goddess gave of her body, she gifted the bird her tendons, which he used to string his treasured lute.

After the insurrection by the treacherous [[Feall Sisters]] and their [[Dramhaíl Coven]], Adaiwen went into hiding like the other members, disappearing from public view to evade the sister's wrath.

# Reemergence

Unsatisfied with the poverty and hardship that continually challenged the people of the valley, Adaiwen turned to an old prophecy from the [[Cthonic Gods|Cthonic]] goddess [[Zell]] that told of seven pure-hearted warriors that would bring light to the realm called [[The Bastards of Light]]. He spent decades wandering, seeking out the wisest and most capable of the denizens of Raiz. Having assembled his group of heroes, he sent them to right the wrongs of the valley, while he, himself, set to dismantle a malicious cult that had taken root in the remote village of [[Aldeia de Espiga]].

Adopting the alias of [[Pica Pau]], Adaiwen embedded himself in the village for years, earning trust and bringing joy to those who labored on the land. Unfortunately, his stewardship of the population served to be his undoing, as a venemous child from the [[Família do Corvinho]] corrupted him using dark mycelial magic, transforming him from a stalwart protector to the [[Beast in the East]].

In this twisted form, Adaiwen was slain by his successors - those who took up the mantle as the bastards. His body was tipped into a fissure in the land, where it burned up in the lava before the land sealed itself, ending the blight that suffocated the rural village.
