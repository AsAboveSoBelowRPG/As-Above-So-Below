During the age of elementals in the [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] realm of Mag Duinn, Fíonnuar was the first Naiad to bring water to the pocket. She was such a profoundly powerful water spirit, that she created the great lake of Loch Linne when she was born.

Since then, she has given birth to all the Naiads in the land. Unlike other Naiads, she is a gentle lover of mortal beings, and delights in their presence along her banks. Those who know her can't fathom how she managed to give birth to spirits like [[Melusina]] and [[Piedá]].

# Snáithe Airgid

[[Nespera]] appointed Fíonnuar to the holy coven [[Snáithe Airgid]], which was established to govern the realm. There were no objections to this appointment, especially from [[Limpanos]] who was delighted to have her old friend join the sisterhood.

Wishing to give her an appropriate gift during the great giving, Nespera entrusted the elemental with Her blood. 

After the [[Feall Sisters]] brought their insurrection to the coven, Fíonnuar returned to her ancestral home, beneath the waters of [[Loch Linne]]. There she supported her friends [[Afonydd]] and [[Vagar]].
