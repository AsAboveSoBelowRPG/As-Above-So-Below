The youngest of the [[Snáithe Airgid]], and the last to be admitted to the holy council, Bancott is a gifted seer who's abilities rival those of the [[Cthonic Gods|Cthonic]] [[Demigods|Demigod]] [[Zell]]. Though she is gifted in foreseeing future events, she is a woman without a past, as she has no memory of her life before she was brought before the [[Rustic God|Goddess]] [[Nespera]].

It is said that the Goddess was deeply protective of Bancott and, perhaps aside from [[Alcochete]], spent the most time mentoring the child. Because she was coddled by the goddess and given special attention, the others in the coven perceived her as naive and fragile, though there was no animosity between them. Everyone treated her as if she were their natural born sister.

When Nespera gave of her body, she gave her gift to Bancott in private; what she was offered has never been revealed.

# Insurrection

Bancott knew the treason the [[Feall Sisters]] would enact long before even they formed the idea. Though she tried to warn [[Nespera]], the Goddess told the young witch that she simply saw a possible future, and regardless, it wasn't their place to change the course of the future. If it was meant to be so, it will manifest regardless.

And so it did; [[Colma]], [[Fabhtanna]] and [[Easóg]] formed their own wicked [[Dramhaíl Coven]] and scattered the Airgid into hiding and isolation. The Goddess, herself, took Bancott into hiding - marking the last time she was seen in the realm.
