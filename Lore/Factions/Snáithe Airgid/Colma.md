Colma is the eldest of the [[Feall Sisters]]; said to be the most beautiful and most treacherous of the triplets. She is one of the most notorious villains of the [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] realm of Mag Duinn, as the mastermind of the uprising that saw the end of the coven that ruled over the lands by the will of the goddess [[Nespera]].

# Snáithe Airgid

Colma and her sisters [[Fabhtanna]] and [[Easóg]] were elected to the coven of the [[Snáithe Airgid]] by virtue of a prophecy made by goddess [[Zell]]. At the time, the coven numbered seven members, and a reading of the prophecy seemed to imply that, by taking the sisters in and mentoring them, a great disaster might be averted.

For decades this seemed to be the case - the coven functioned peacefully and brought great prosperity to a challenging environment. Colma took the sky as her domain and served the coven by sending whispers on the wind, allowing the group to communicate over great distances. When the gifting occurred, Nespera gave Colma her wing.

# Uprising

The entire time she served on the coven, a darkness was fomenting inside her - one she shared with her sisters. Together the three of them plotted to overthrow the Airgid and replace the peaceful rule with their wrath. Renouncing their vows in secret, the three reformed as the [[Dramhaíl Coven]] and began to undermine their former sisterhood. Colma's role was to twist the communications on the wind, to intercept sensitive communications, to spread false rumors and to listen to the vulnerable secrets of the others.

# War

After scattering the Airgid, the triplets would have only had to contest with the Goddess Nespera for control of Mag Duinn, save for one fact they failed to take into account: the three sisters hated each other. The three fought each other relentlessly until the coming of [[Nazerack]] brought the calamity to the realm.

Since this time, the infighting among the Dramhaíl seems to have ceased. Those who live in the crossfire find this as troubling as the war.
