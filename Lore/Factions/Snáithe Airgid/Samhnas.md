Born in the wildest of woods in the realm of Mag Duinn, Samhnas was a natural trickster since her youth. Possessing of a natural talent for magic and illusion, Samhnas found herself using these gifts to beguile  the mortal settlers that began to establish themselves in the [[Vale de Raiz]].

Seeing the potential in her abilities, [[Alcochete]] brought the satyr before the others on the [[Snáithe Airgid]], who all emphatically approved of the troublemaker after she delighted them with a one-woman play; a performance that displayed her natural charisma and subtly showcased her powerful illusory magics.

Working with the Airgid, she tempered her hate for the settlements and worked to help them manage their natural spaces, eventually taking the [[The Outlands]] where she was born as her domain. When [[Nespera]] gave of her body, she gifted Samhnas her hair.

# Exile

When the [[Dramhaíl Coven]] shattered the Airgid, Samhnas went into hiding like the other members, seeking to hide themselves from the sinister [[Feall Sisters]] that betrayed their sisterhood. Since then, no one has seen her, though rumors say that the faun and [[Satyr]] of the Outlands are her offspring.
