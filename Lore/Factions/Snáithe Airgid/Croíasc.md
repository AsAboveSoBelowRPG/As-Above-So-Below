When the realm of Mag Duinn was still learning to accept life, much of it's geographical features had yet to be established. When the [[naiad]] [[Fíonnuar]] sparked and she and her children began to bring water to the [[Vale de Raiz]], it had the unintended consequence of flooding the lowest parts of the valley - those who weren't able to escape the torrent were lost to the tides.

One prominent denizen of Old Mag Duinn was a powerful and beautiful witch named Croíasc who built her home in the deepest, darkest part of the valley. When the waters came, she was trapped and drowned in her home. Her body lay submerged beneath the murky swamp for centuries, and, while most forgot her name and her face, Fíonnuar never did - often visiting the preserved corpse to pay her respects.

As the [[Rustic God|Goddess]] [[Nespera]] began to assemble a coven to rule the land, the naiad was elected to the council. Here, she made an appeal to the others, and to the Goddess, to right the wrong that befell Croíasc. The others agreed and Nespera brought the body to the surface. Though she had been submerged in the icy marsh for hundreds of years, there was still a spark of life inside, which the goddess fanned back to life by blowing her life-giving breath over the body. 

Returning from the cusp of death only sharpened her powers, and that insight made her unique amongst those who wove the old magics of the realm. It was unanimously agreed that she should join the [[Snáithe Airgid]]. 

Though she was, again, alive, her body was in terrible disrepair, so when Nespera made a gift of her body, she gave Croíasc her skin.

# Insurrection

After the betrayal of [[Colma]] and her sisters, Croíasc went into hiding like the rest of the Airgid. Many tell stories of seeing her floating amongst the bayous and marshlands, but the [[Slíomach Bog]] is a common dumping ground for corpses, and none of these sightings have been confirmed.
