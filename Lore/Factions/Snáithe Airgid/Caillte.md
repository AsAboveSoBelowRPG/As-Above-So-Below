Early in the development of Mag Duinn, Caillte joined the cult of [[Nespera]] and embarked on an endless pilgrimage to spread Her word across the realm. In this way, she became the first priestess of the new religion and one of the goddesses favorite children.

# Snáithe Airgid

When Nespera sought to establish a coven to rule over the land, there was no doubt that there should be a seat for her most devout priestess. Caillte joined the coven only after she was assured it wouldn't preclude her from her missionary work.

Like her sister in the coven, [[Alcochete]], Caillte chose no land to govern, opting instead to traverse the lands and spread the word of [[The Ashen Bough]] - and, in many ways, it was over religion that Caillte came to preside.

When Nespera came to the Coven with the great gifting, the Goddes bestowed upon her Her right eye, so she would always know the true path to follow. Caillte was so touched by this gesture that she pulled out her own eye and cast it into the sky, replacing it with Nespera's own.

After the [[Feall Sisters]] staged their revolt, Caillte fled from public view, effectively vanishing. Considering she hasn't been seen since the insurrection, many believe her to have been slain by the wretched sisters.

# Legacy

The paths that Caillte blazed in her endless pilgrimage became popular thoroughfares, which have since evolved into the roads and paths still used in the realm to this day.

The Eye of Caillte still hangs in the perpetually black sky of the realm, shining like the only star in an empty abyss. Travelers in the realm still use it as a landmark for navigation. Paired with the network of roads she established, Caillte has become the patron of wanderers within Mag Duinn.
