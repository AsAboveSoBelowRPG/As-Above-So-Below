During the earliest days of Mag Duinn, the elementals were the only inhabitants. It was through the whims of these beings that the realm took the shape it carries now. Limpanos was one of those that helped seed the dense and enigmatic woodlands of [[Cthonic Remains|Cthonic]] pocket.

# Snáthe Airgid

When the Goddess [[Nespera]] sought to assemble a coven of just and powerful beings to rule over Mag Duinn, she personally picked Limpanos as a member, appointing her second, after the sorceress [[Alcochete]]. 

The Goddess admired her endless compassion and her dedication to abundance - despite the darkness of the realm, Limpanos always tended to the trees that provided life to the critters of her woods. In this way, even if life was dangerous and bleak, there would always remain some hope and happiness.

As such, during the great gifting, Nespera bestowed her foot to Limpanos to remind her to always stay grounded, but mobile.

After the betrayal of the [[Feall Sisters]], Limpanos fled her territory in Sliogán and wandered the realm. Reported sightings of the dryad were marked in numerous corners of the realm, including [[The Outlands]] and [[Fásaigh Sangrenta]], though these accounts tapered off dramatically after a few dozen years.
