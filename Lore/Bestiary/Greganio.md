Greganio is a giant known to terrorize the spires of the [[Upper Plateau]] province of Breahnhelle. Ironically, through his centuries of pillaging noble estates, he actually gained a sense of sophistication - learning that he need only threaten the people and make demands to get what he wanted, rather than by actually causing the destruction he promised. As such, he became lazy and spoiled.

A young woman named Maria Leeds, a lesser noble seeking to improve her station among the elite, decided that if she could dominate the giant, she would gain tremendous influence in society. Armed with nothing more than her resolve, she ascended to the beasts cave and belittled him until he was a sobbing mess, then demanded that he mate with her. Helpless against the tiny woman's demands, he did as she said.

The two managed to procreate, though they would never see each other again. Greganio stopped raiding the houses of the spires as per Maria's demands, and because he hasn't left the spire that houses his den since their meeting, isn't aware that she died giving birth to their daughter [[Fianna Leeds]].

It has been so long since Greganio last raided the nobles that he has become something of a mythical beast, with many claiming the old stories of his attacks were little more than folk tales told to scare the children of the nobility.
