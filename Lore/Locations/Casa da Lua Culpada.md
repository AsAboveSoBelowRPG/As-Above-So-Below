Among the noble estates of [[Upper Plateau]] province of [[Ard Breahnhelle]], [[Mag Duinn]], Lua Culpada is by far the largest and most elegant. Built on a peak below the towering [[Spion Isolte]], it boasts many natural and engineered feats. This castle has long been the home of the Sintaflores bloodline, the rulers of the province. Most recently, it has been owned by the Duque and Duquesa [[Ronaldo Sintaflores|Ronaldo]] and [[Catarina Sintaflores]].

The manor is renowned for its stunning architecture, and was built over the province's primary spring, which feeds a massive bathhouse. A lift station connects the estate directly to the upper plateau and the [[Vale de Raiz]] below.

# Masquerade

Having had a long friendship with the owner of the [[Sanatório Desesperança]], [[Isabel Salinas|Mistress Isabel]], Duque [[Ronaldo Sintaflores]] decided to host a massive annual fundraiser known as [[The Lament Masquerade]]. While it somehow manages to raise more money than it costs to host the party every year, the charitable framework is only a flimsy excuse to throw a debaucherous party with the other noble families of the Upper Plateau. It's popularity is such that even the Lord of the Land, [[Salvadaro Harbin Glumbo]] attends regularly, as does the [[Cthonic Gods|Cthonic]] goddess of fortune, [[Zell]].

# Amenities

The palace is an architectural wonder that begs to be explored. While many have only heard tales of such opulence, guests of the Sintaflores family, or those lucky enough to attend the Masquerade are invited to explore the manor as they please. Don't miss these features:

- **Verandas:** Until the establishment of [[Novo Ayodar]], Lua Culpada was the highest palace in the entire realm, and two massive verandas were built to best enjoy the view of the province. On a clear day, it is said you can survey the entire realm from these balconies.
- **Ballroom:** The showpiece of the masquerade, the grand ballroom greets visitors with its crystal chandeliers and exotic tapestries. Though it has never reached full capacity, it is said to be able to accommodate hundreds of people.
- **Chapel of the Daughter:** A newly constructed temple is fitted with a gorgeous statue of the Daughter of the Pale Blossom carved by the late Eva De Cabra. This is a quiet place of worship and reflection for [[The Groundskeepers|those who worship Her]].
- **Audience Chamber:** Built as a space to welcome those coming to petition the Duque; the chamber mostly stands as a symbolic link to the people of the province at this moment.
- **Spring Garden:** A natural garden has been cultivated around the source of the palace's spring. Lovingly tended, it provides a quiet respite from the rocky expanse outside the walls.
- **Guest Suites:** An entire wing of the palace is dedicated to housing guests and providing a sense of home to the weary travelers who come to visit. These chambers often fill up fast during the Masquerade, though people rarely attend to sleep alone.
- **The Grand Bathhouse:** Natural spring water is heated and channeled into a majestic bathhouse with two medicinal baths and saunas.
- **The Noble Apartments:** These rooms are reserved for the Sintaflores Family and their close guests. [[Isabel Salinas]] retains a permanent room in this wing.

# Staff

- **Jackard Banbhán**: The head butler of the estate, Jackard has been a loyal employee since Duque Ronaldo was just a child. He is a humorless and professional servant who both manages the affairs of the staff as well as the extravagant parties hosted by the Sintaflores.
- **Faolan Cíen**: Executive chef of the estate, Faolan manages that kitchen staff of 6 full time cooks that work shifts around the clock. Faolan is a jovial, stout man that delights in seeing people enjoy his recipes. During the yearly festival, he is tasked with hiring up to 20 temporary chefs to supply the feast with unforgettable food.
- **Eavan Glia**: Duquesa Catarina's personal aid. Hired before the Duquesa married into the family, the two of them have been inseparable since Catarina arrived at the estate. She is kind and genuine, though those who cross her would argue otherwise. During the Masquerade she is given the freedom to attend as a guest and the anonymity gives her clearance to live her dreams one night a year.
- **Mac Amhra**: Head of the guard, Mac takes his job incredibly seriously. Much of Mac's year is spent preparing for a single night - the Lament Masquerade. Though he is often tasked with escorting Catarina when she leaves the palace, and is on high alert when guests visit, his primary job is to maintain the peace during the Masquerade.
- **Nuala Ahnrach**: A seamstress of notoriety within the realm, Nuala was hired by the late Ronaldo to furnish extravagant costumes for the noble family during the masquerade. Catarina was so taken with the woman's work that she hired her on full time to keep her wardrobe fresh and full. She is expected to deliver an entirely new selection every season, as the Duquesa rarely wears the same garment twice. 
- **Naisi Nacht**: [[Rhagorol Sintaflores|Principe Rhagorol]]'s mentor, teacher and closest advisor. Naisi was hired by Ronaldo to shape his oldest son into a worthy successor to his rule, and, as such, has been by the prince's side his entire life. Though Rhagorol is now a grown man, he keeps his closest advisor by his side always.
