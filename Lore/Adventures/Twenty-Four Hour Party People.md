When the [[Cthonic Gods|Cthonic]] [[Demigods|Demigod]] [[Zell]] vanished after a private audience with [[Ard Breahnhelle]] noble [[Afonso Roche]], her spool-bearer [[Vyolante A'Dora]] sought the aid of her sister's husband, Lord of the Land [[Salvadaro Harbin Glumbo]]. Finding himself stripped of his supernatural powers, he instead sent [[The Bastards of Light]] to the [[Upper Plateau]] to investigate the disappearance.

While their objective is to seek answers from the mysterious knight of [[Novo Ayodar]], the annual fundraiser [[The Lament Masquerade]] - a fundraiser for the [[Sanatório Desesperança]] on the [[Ilha Lamento]] - happened to be taking place: an event Afonso attends every year without fail. Thinking they might safely confront the hero in a public venue, the adventurers took to the heights of [[Mag Duinn]] to seek answers and indulge in the fineries of high society.

# The Heartbroken Sorcerer

Following the high road out of [[Aldeia de Raiz]], the group had to pass by the clifftop ruins of [[Theda Vidigueira|Theda's]] former haunt - the [[The De Cabra Murders|The D'Albergia Estate]]. Surprisingly, they encountered a grieving old man who claimed to be a former lover of the hag. Though he spoke lovingly of her, he also acknowledged that she was a complicated woman who suffered the repercussions of her many evil deeds. Suspiciously, though, he referred to the woman as [[Easóg]] - a name that didn't align with any identity known to the group.

The old man, a former legendary sorcerer named [[Pedro Pipocas]], inquired as to whether the group was on their way to the Masquerade, though their colorful costumes seemed to give their intentions away. In an attempt to shake the old man from following them to the high society event, they made a wager that he wouldn't be able to save three villages in the realm in under 20 minutes. They were quite surprised when he emerged from a silver portal with three precious treasures:

- **The Eternal Ember of Caorthainn**: Encased in a glass vessel, this ever-glowing ember shines bright like a lantern, and burns hot enough to start a fire in any weather conditions if removed from its container.
- **The Blessed Bell of Nadroath:** A beautifully engraved brass bell that wears the heavy patina of age. Ancient runes ring the circumference of the lip. When rung, it sings with the beautiful voice of a woman rather than the metallic clang of a bell. 
- **Silver Soul of Dullahan:** Eons ago, a troublesome Lich named Dullahan troubled the people of Cill Anfich. Before they rose up against him, he created a phylactery in a broach shaped like a silver feather - to this day it is said to contain his soul.

Each treasure was gifted to him after preventing these remote village from coming to ruin. He left each with the adventurers in hopes that they may come in use as they sought to do good in the realm. Having thus proved his worth, [[Errndar Bearfinder]] held his end of the bargain and carried the old man to the lift station, whereas [[Pip Vellum]] became convinced that the old man was a gold dragon in disguise.

# Ascending and Entering

The group arrived moments before the final lift was about to make way up the steep cliffside. In the moments before they boarded, they had an opportunity to meet a couple of fellow revelers, who claimed to be approaching the [[Casa da Lua Culpada]] from the [[Vale de Raiz]] due to a yearly tradition where the couple spends the week preceding the masquerade living in poverty, so they fineries of the celebration are that much more enjoyable. None of the Bastards particularly cared for the gentleman's attitude towards the disenfranchised people of the valley, though they maintained a cordial attitude with the man. His female companion remained silent during the whole of their encounter.

Before they boarded the lift, Errnie took the shape of a rat and hopped into [[Kadav Papazuzian|Kadav's]] pocket to sneak into the festival without a ticket. Thus armed with two legitimate invitations from Sal and Magda, Kadav's invitation to join the band and Pip's counterfeit ticket, the group was poised to join the festivities without causing alarm.

Drama seemed to find the group before they even stepped foot off the lift, as an old man who seemed to be more tree than man confronted those who rode the right-side lift from [[Ard Breahnhelle]]; claiming that he knew their true identity, giving an absurd and seemingly made-up name. When he was predictably wrong, his arm stiffened as the curse of the Masquerade hardened him - the existing damage to his body seemed to suggest that this wasn't the first time he engaged in this stunt. Surprisingly, though, he wasn't accusing the group, but the strangers they rode with.

An onlooker informed them that every year this old man [[Blantól Bhachas]] waits for his brother to step food onto the platform and then accosts him with some ridiculous name, despite knowing exactly who he is. His bizarre behavior is attributed to dementia and, though he is committing one of the major taboos of the event, is disregarded as a harmless eccentric. Many even use his outburst as the unofficial start of the masquerade and gather to watch it play out.

The head guard, Mac Amhra, greeted the group politely, asking for their tickets. Kadav entered with Apalonia and [[Elgor Silvershadow]] and [[Morgamorgar]] entered with the Glumbo's legitimate tickets - however, when doing so, Mac revealed that the wax seal on the invitations concealed a hidden security feature: a thin metal coin stamped with a serial number. Luckily, Pipocas happily claimed Pip as his personal guest, using his signet ring as a proof of his identity. And, just like that, the group was admitted to high society and dispersed amongst the crowd.

# Answers and Intrigue

Looking to draw attention away from themselves, the Bastards split up to seek answers and explore the amenities of the masquerade. Pip established telepathic communications amongst the party, save for Errnie, who was still rat shaped, and each left to pursue their own agendas.

Kadav spared no time in mingling with the attendees, though he was immediately confronted by a strange woman who staged a chance collision with him, whispering that she was being watched and it was not safe to speak openly - and that he was to bid on her in the human auction. Secretly she dropped a sack of coins into his apron pocket, nearly crushing the transformed Errnie. Directly afterwards, he found himself speaking with the foolish old man from the entry platform - Blantól. The old man divulged that he believed his brother was replaced by a demonic entity decades ago, and has been attempting to uncover the fiend's true identity since. While Kadav was made uncomfortable about the talk of demonic antics, the old man proved he was no fool when he announced he knew Kadav's identity, slipping him a paper detailing a lower Cthonic demon named [[Pazu Pazuzu|Pazu]]. 

Attempting to blend in with the crowd and indulge in the fineries he was promised back in the valley, Morga quickly slipped into the crowd where he quickly wooed a beautiful reveler only to leave her alone as he left to work the room. However, rather than finding pleasures of the flesh, he found himself in church, listening to a sermon from [[Irmã Tempestade]], the itinerant priestess bringing the philosophy of [[The Groundskeepers]] to the realm. Here he was pestered by a fundamentalist who claimed he wished to convert those who still clung to [[The Ashen Bough]] by force, yet only provided a stack of pamphlets when Morga pressed him on his means and methods.

Errnie, having escaped from Kadav's pocket after a near death encounter with a heavy sack of coins, sought to eavesdrop on distracted revelers while he still held his rodent form. In a hallway he heard two attendees, dressed in the rustic greens of the Sintaflores loyalists, spoke about a secret mass taking place during the auction. Though Errnie followed one of them into the courtyard garden, he lost them when they snuck into a bramble.

Pip, who first witnessed a group of appalling nobles playing at ruling a province during The Fool's Court, realized that he had infinitely more in common with the staff at the Masquerade than the others in attendance, and made quick friends with a humble commoner from [[Ard Breahnhelle]], who informed the adventurer that he would be a welcome guest in his village if he should ever pass through. Slipping into the kitchen after an enlightening conversation, Pip was shocked to find that, despite the kitchen being fully equipped and stocked, there were no cooks or chefs preparing food. The servers and cleaners informed him that when food was needed, it simply appeared. Seeking to ease their burden, Pip helped with the dishes and worked subtly to urge the servants towards rebellion.

Elsewhere, Elgor stepped outside to examine the makeshift zoo set up by Afonso's [[Beast Warden|Wardens]]. While the display was an exotic menagerie of rare specimens, it was clear that the animals were highly distressed and abused. One of the creatures was able to speak, and informed Elgor that he was a sentient being and didn't belong in a cage, begging the orc for assistance in escaping the confines. He didn't pay much attention to the plight of the creature until he saw the massive snow beast freaking out in the central cage. He weighed the laws of the Masquerade, and, not seeing "releasing creatures from the zoo" listed as a punishable offense, set to trying to free the strange plant man.
