## Questions for PCs

1. How do you think you'll die?
2. What do you feel most grateful for in your life?
3. If you could change a mistake you made in your past, what would it be and how would you do it differently?
4. What color best describes your personality and why?
5. What do you see yourself doing when you retire?
6. What's the most fulfilling accomplishment you've had in life?
7. What are you looking for in a partner?
8. How do you have fun?
9. What is your idea of a romantic evening?
10. How would you impress your date's parents?
11. Describe a time when you took care of a loved one.
12. You have just been given a curse by a wretched hag and have only one day to live. How do you spend it?
13. What makes you laugh?
14. What do you find most offensive in life?
15. A commoner bumps you in the market, smudging pig filth on your velvet cloak - What do you do?
16. If you could have three wishes, what would they be?
17. If you could be a circus performer which act would you be?
18. What do you hate about dating?
19. What would you change about yourself?
